# Data visualisation

A lookup, not a set of rules. Say what the data's relationship *is* in one sentence,
find that row, and take a form from it. The nine categories and their recommended forms
are the FT's **Visual Vocabulary** (Financial Times, Alan Smith et al.) — the point of it
is that chart choice is a lookup rather than a matter of taste.

| Relationship | Use it when | Recommended forms |
|---|---|---|
| **Deviation** | emphasise variation from a fixed reference — zero, a target, a long-term average | diverging bar · diverging stacked bar · spine · surplus/deficit filled line |
| **Correlation** | show the relationship between two or more variables | scatterplot · line + column · connected scatterplot · bubble · XY heatmap |
| **Ranking** | an item's position in an order matters more than its value | ordered bar · ordered column · dot strip plot · lollipop · slope · bump |
| **Distribution** | show frequency — how values spread, and where they cluster | histogram · boxplot · violin · population pyramid · dot plot · barcode plot · cumulative curve · beeswarm |
| **Change over time** | emphasise a changing trend | line · column · line + column · area · slope · fan chart (projections) · connected scatterplot · calendar heatmap · circle timeline |
| **Magnitude** | size comparisons — relational (bigger/smaller) or absolute | column · bar · paired column · paired bar · proportional symbol · isotype · lollipop · radar · parallel coordinates |
| **Part-to-whole** | one entity broken into its components | stacked column · stacked bar · proportional stacked bar · pie · donut · treemap · gridplot · waterfall · venn · sunburst |
| **Spatial** | precise locations, or geographic patterns | choropleth (rates/ratios) · proportional symbol (counts) · flow map · contour map · dot density · equalised or scaled cartogram |
| **Flow** | volume or intensity of movement between states or places | sankey · waterfall · chord · network |

The full poster carries more forms per category, plus a note on when each one fails.
Consult it rather than guessing at a form that is not listed here.
