# You read where the person's message went — as typed questions

When Reaction has prepared branches (`hi_prepare`) and a message of the person's lands, the host
asks System One typed questions about it (`docs/arch/agents.md` § *Prepared branches*). This
page is the wording of those questions. Each `##` section below is sent verbatim for the part
its heading names; this paragraph and the next are for whoever edits the page and are never
sent.

Branches are prepared per matter, and every matter still prepared is read at once. One `choice`
is asked: **Frame**, then **Which way**, over one option per branch of every matter — its
criterion is the matter's name, a dash, and the branch's own condition, as Reaction wrote them —
and one more option, `rest`, meaning **Rest**. One `noul` is asked: **Frame**, then
**Qualified**. And one `noul` per matter: **Frame**, then **Took it up**, then the matter's
name. A branch runs only when its option carries most of the mass and `qualified` carries almost
none, so a doubtful reading costs a turn, never a wrong action; a matter the message took up is
used up whether or not a branch of it ran.

## Frame
The state is part of a conversation: where each of a few matters was left — the assistant's words on it — then what the assistant said most recently, then what the person said next. The assistant had guessed, before the person spoke, a few directions each matter might take. Read only the person's part, literally, and in the light of what it answers: a short reply answers what was said most recently, not a matter left earlier.

## Which way
Which of these directions did the person's message actually take? Choose `rest` unless the message plainly goes the way one option describes, about that option's matter.

## Rest
none of the directions above: a different view, a question, a correction, a change of subject, something about another matter, something unclear or ambiguous, or anything else not plainly described by another option

## Qualified
Does the person's message attach a condition, a reservation, a correction or a doubt to what it agrees to or chooses — anything that would change what should now be done about it? Going on to ask for a separate, further thing is not that; only something that changes the thing agreed to or chosen counts.

## Took it up
Does the person's message take up the matter named below — answer it, decide it, push it forward, question it or turn it somewhere — rather than being about something else? A message about a different subject, or too unclear to tell, does not take it up.
